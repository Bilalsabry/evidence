## Foundational (read in this order)

### Rashkin et al., 2023 — "Measuring Attribution in Natural Language Generation Models"

**Why first:** the AIS framework. Source of the modern attribution conversation. You're decomposing what they treat as a single label.

**Citation:** Rashkin, H., Nikolaev, V., Lamm, M., Aroyo, L., Collins, M., Das, D., Petrov, S., Tomar, G. S., Turc, I., Reitter, D. (2023). Measuring Attribution in Natural Language Generation Models. *TACL.*

### Bohnet et al., 2022 — "Attributed Question Answering"

**Why second:** defines the task ("attributed QA") you're operating in.

**Citation:** Bohnet, B., Tran, V. Q., Verga, P., Aharoni, R., Andor, D., Soares, L. B., Eisenstein, J., Ganchev, K., Herzig, J., Hui, K., Kwiatkowski, T., Ma, J., Ni, J., Saralegui, T. Attributed Question Answering: Evaluation and Modeling for Attributed Large Language Models. *arXiv:2212.08037.*

[notes go here]

### Gao et al., 2023 — "Enabling Large Language Models to Generate Text with Citations" (ALCE)

**Why third — and most important.** This is your closest comparison point. ALCE is the most-cited attribution benchmark. The Week 5 ALCE-subset experiment defends against the "did you compare?" reviewer comment.

**Citation:** Gao, T., Yen, H., Yu, J., Chen, D. (2023). Enabling Large Language Models to Generate Text with Citations. *EMNLP.*

[notes go here]

---

### Liu et al., 2023 — "Evaluating Verifiability in Generative Search Engines"

**Why fourth:** the "is the citation actually verifiable" question, closest in spirit to your three rules.

**Citation:** Liu, N. F., Zhang, T., Liang, P. (2023). Evaluating Verifiability in Generative Search Engines. *Findings of EMNLP.*

[notes go here]

## Adjacent (skim; one paragraph each)

### Menick et al., 2022 — "Teaching Language Models to Support Answers with Verified Quotes" (GopherCite)

Closest prior art on span-level citing. Cites with quotes generated by the model — but doesn't validate them at inference time. **The "model generates the quote" vs. "model points at a span the system already has" distinction is worth a sentence in our related work.**

**Citation:** Menick, J., Trebacz, M., Mikulik, V., Aslanides, J., Song, F., Chadwick, M., Glaese, M., Young, S., Campbell-Gillingham, L., Irving, G., McAleese, N. (2022). Teaching language models to support answers with verified quotes. *arXiv:2203.11147.*

[notes go here]

---

## Methodologically useful

### Es et al., 2023 — RAGAS

Evaluation framework for RAG. Their "faithfulness" metric is a single number; we position the three-rule decomposition as the right granularity for frameworks like this to adopt.

**Citation:** Es, S., James, J., Espinosa-Anke, L., Schockaert, S. (2023). RAGAS: Automated Evaluation of Retrieval Augmented Generation. *arXiv:2309.15217.*

[notes go here]
