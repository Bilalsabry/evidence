## What we still owe

Two items remain genuinely open. We name them here rather than let a reviewer find them.

- **Human seed-audit sample.** v2 semantic faithfulness was spot-audited (3 labels) plus the full v1 false-refusal audit — not an exhaustive human pass over all 254. The conservative path is a stratified random sample of 50 seeds (mix of fact types), with a second-rater Cohen's κ on ~30 of them to retire the "no IAA" attack. The protocol is written; results will be recorded in the datasheet. See [`SUBMISSION_RUNBOOK.md` Phase 2](SUBMISSION_RUNBOOK.md) and [`fda_label_bench_PROVENANCE.md` "Known limitations"](fda_label_bench_PROVENANCE.md).
- **Natural-failure human grading (§5.6).** The benchmark uses injected failures; the realism check is real LLM outputs (no injection) graded by humans into the taxonomy. The protocol is pre-written and explicit that the harness does NOT auto-grade — humans label, the harness joins. Marked `[OPEN — not run]` everywhere; no number is fabricated. See [`natural-failure-protocol.md`](natural-failure-protocol.md) and [`SUBMISSION_RUNBOOK.md` Phase 3](SUBMISSION_RUNBOOK.md).
